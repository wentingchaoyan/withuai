Games with Rules & Winning/Losing

When your child can't follow the rules

The rules may be too many or too complex. Start with games that have just one rule (like simply drawing in turns) and build up experience by repeating the same game. Rather than scolding when they break a rule, saying "You waited your turn!" the moment they follow it helps it stick.

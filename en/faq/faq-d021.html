Self-Care (Toilet, Dressing & Hygiene)

Getting dressed takes a long time / my child doesn't know the order

Getting dressed is a series of complex movements (which clothes, what order, which is front or back...). Start with big buttons or zippers with big pulls, keep them within sight, hold one side steady, and so on—break the steps down and go little by little.

What if my child's pronunciation is unclear?

There's no need to make them say it again. Without pointing out your child's pronunciation, casually reply using the correct sounds yourself ("anpan" → "Yes, Anpanman!"). For children whose speech tends to be unclear because of the structure or strength of their mouth muscles, continuing to let them hear the correct sounds is the best practice of all.

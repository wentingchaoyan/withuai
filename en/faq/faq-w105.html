Equal Relationships

Are you not allowed to get angry with or say "no" to a child with a disability?

Teach that even with a disability, it's okay to say "no" as an equal friend. Making them hold back specially prevents an equal relationship from growing. But add that a hurtful way of speaking isn't used with anyone.

For a child, you could say: "You can say 'no' to anyone about things you don't like. Just don't use a mean way of speaking with any child."

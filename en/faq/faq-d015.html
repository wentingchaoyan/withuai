Remembering & Finding (Memory)

My child quickly forgets what they've heard

Auditory memory is often harder than visual memory. Adding visual support, like showing picture cards, photos, or real objects while you talk, keeping sequences to two steps, or using songs and rhythm, makes things easier to remember.

Eating & Drinking (Feeding)

Which cup should we start with? When do we introduce straws?

There isn't a single fixed order for cups; choose based on posture, flow rate, and ease of holding. The numbers in the diagram are reference numbers to explain the options, not a fixed developmental order.
1. If the head tends to tip backward: a U-shaped cutout cup where only the nose-side rim is lower.
2. If it's hard to hold the cup: a two-handled cup or one with a wide base.
3. To practice "sipping" while reducing spills: a valve-free free-flow cup.
4. If your child can tilt it on their own: a small open cup.
A straw isn't a stage that must come after the open cup; it can be tried in parallel as a separate track of skill. If choking, coughing, a wet-sounding voice, or changes in breathing continue, don't push the practice forward. Consult your doctor or a professional who handles feeding and swallowing.

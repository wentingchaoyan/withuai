When your child knocks things down right away

Knocking down is easier, and the result is more dramatic and fun. First, let them enjoy being the "knock-it-down" person to their heart's content, turning it into a back-and-forth where the adult builds and the child knocks down. Before long, add in a bit of building with "let's put one more on top, then knock it down," gradually increasing their part in building.

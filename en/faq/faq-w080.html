Overnight & Camp

What preparation is needed for my child to join a camp or overnight activity without a parent?

An overnight without a parent is a big step in independence. As preparation, convey the schedule, place, belongings, and the bathing and bedtime flow concretely with photos or an itinerary. Getting used to it from short stays in stages, sharing medication, meals, and sensory accommodations and how to handle trouble with the organizer beforehand, and deciding on reliable staff and a signal all bring reassurance. Choosing a camp for children with disabilities or an accommodating general program is also effective. Take on the challenge starting within a manageable range.

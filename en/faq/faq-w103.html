Handling Unwanted Actions

What should I do when they won't stop even after I say "stop"?

Because it may not get through the first time, teach saying "stop" clearly once more, leaving the spot, and telling an adult. Make clear they don't have to keep enduring it.

For a child, you could say: "Say 'stop' clearly one more time, and if that doesn't work, tell an adult right away. You don't have to just put up with it."

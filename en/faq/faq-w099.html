Why Learning Differs & Accommodations

Why is only ○○ allowed to rest or move during class?

Explain that some children need time to move or rest because staying still is hard, and that it's a needed accommodation, not special treatment.

For a child, you could say: "Sometimes you can't concentrate without moving your body. That's why there's time to move a little or take a break."

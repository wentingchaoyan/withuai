Founder's Story

My child was born with Down syndrome.

The first time I held him, his tiny hand gripped my finger. In that moment, I made a promise to do everything I could for this child. Every time I saw his smile, I felt an overwhelming desire to help him reach his full potential.

But the reality was harder than I ever imagined.

My child's growth is slow. There were nights when I couldn't help comparing him to other children, crushed by anxiety and impatience. "Where is he right now in his development?" "Where is he heading?" "What can I do for him today?"—I carried these unanswered questions with me constantly.

I wanted to consult with his therapists, but I couldn't convey his daily progress well. At the hospital, I repeated the same explanations every time. Family and caregivers were disconnected—no one saw the whole picture. I wanted to celebrate his small victories with someone, but that "someone" wasn't there. I felt so alone.

Specialists came in with preconceptions based on limited samples, already convinced that "this child can't do this." At home, I watched his small changes every day. He had grown so much in six months—yet standardized assessment tools said "no progress." The child I saw at home and the child in those evaluations felt like two different people. I desperately wanted to share what I observed at home.

One day, I visited a special needs school. What I saw there cut deep into my heart. After graduation, the options were predetermined jobs and minimal wages. That was presented as "reality." Nothing had even started yet—was my child's future already decided? I couldn't stop the tears.

"The future isn't something that happens by default.
It's something we can design."

I've spent many years working in IT. I've always believed that technology can maximize human potential, connect people, and create positive cycles.

That's why I decided to build With U. As a first step toward designing that future.

It's okay if growth is slow. What matters is that everyone involved observes the child, notices the small changes, shares them, and celebrates together. That joy leads to the next step of growth. I wanted to create that cycle.

"Where is he in his development right now?" "Where is he heading?" "What can we do today?"—the app gives you hints. And then families, therapists, and schools can all look in the same direction, supporting the child as One Team.

What he couldn't do yesterday, he did today. We record that moment and celebrate together—"That's amazing!" That joy gives both the child and those supporting him the strength to keep moving forward.

We don't want the next family to experience the loneliness we went through. We want to build a world where no one has to carry this burden alone.

We believe that every small step leads to a bigger future.

Will you join us in expanding your child's possibilities with With U?

— Founder, With U

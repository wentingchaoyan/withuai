Development Support Platform

A central platform for recording and managing your child's growth. Easily document daily activities and get a complete view of their development.

  • Stage-based record management
  • Photo, video, and text recording
  • Overview of overall development
  • Goal setting and tracking

AI automatically analyzes your child's movements from video. Quantifies motor development like walking and sitting, and visualizes changes over time.

  • Posture and movement analysis from video
  • Quantification of motor development
  • Visualization of changes over time
  • Expert verification of results

Growth Record Sharing

Securely share records with family, therapy centers, schools, and medical institutions. Everyone can collaborate with the same information.

  • Secure sharing with caregivers
  • Detailed access permissions
  • Team information coordination
  • Sharing history management

Individual Support Reports

Experts create personalized reports from accumulated records. Provides specific support recommendations tailored to your child.

  • Expert-created reports
  • Specific support recommendations
  • Regular progress reviews
  • Sharing with therapy centers

Growth Magazine

Automatically creates beautiful magazines from recorded photos and videos. A special keepsake to look back on your child's growth.

  • Automatic photo and video layout
  • Monthly and yearly magazines
  • PDF export and printing
  • Sharing with family

Document Management

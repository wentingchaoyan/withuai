AI-Powered Growth Tracking for Your Child's Development

  • Record growth with photos and videos (with tags)
  • AI-powered video analysis for movement development
  • Visualize development with flow diagrams and milestones
  • Scan and store diagnostic reports and therapy plans
  • Invite family members to share growth records

Development Support Platform

A central platform for recording and managing your child's growth. Easily document daily activities and get a complete view of their development.

AI-Powered Development Analysis

AI automatically analyzes your child's movements from video. Quantifies motor development and visualizes changes over time.

Growth Record Sharing

Securely share records with family, therapy centers, schools, and medical institutions for seamless collaboration.

Individual Support Reports

Experts create personalized reports from accumulated records with specific support recommendations.

Growth Magazine

Automatically creates beautiful magazines from recorded photos and videos. A special keepsake of your child's growth.

Document Management

Scan and store documents like diagnostic reports and therapy materials with OCR text recognition.

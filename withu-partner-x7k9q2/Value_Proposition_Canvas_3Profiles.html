Provider · Family · Researcher · Osterwalder format · Mentor discussion artifact · 2026-05-19 · Backed by 00_Strategy_Master_2026Q2/05_Value_Proposition.md v1.1
📍 How to use this canvas: Each profile shows WithU's value proposition (left square) mapped to the stakeholder's customer-segment reality (right circle). Read the right side first ("what they actually need / feel"), then test whether the left side ("what we offer") actually addresses it. The arrow signifies fit — if a Pain Reliever doesn't connect to a real Pain, it's a feature without value. Goals shown twice: Outcome (measurable) and Worldview (meaningful). Same data, two dimensions — see 05 v1.1 Beliefs section.
⏱ Temporal classification: ST Short-term felt value — relief or benefit experienced within hours to ~1 month (acute-moment deliverables, immediate emotional relief) LT Long-term felt value — compounding over months to multi-year (cohort data, lifecycle continuity, career arcs, industry positioning)
💡 Why this matters for the mentor session: Stakeholders evaluate value with different time horizons. Families need ST proof in the first few uses or they leave. Investors / providers / researchers think in LT arcs and discount short-term polish. If a canvas is heavily one-color, that's a signal: are we under-serving the other time horizon for this stakeholder?

✦ OUR WORLDVIEW PROMISE TO PROVIDERS
We see you as professionals whose craft is undervalued by an industry that doesn't track outcomes well. The 49 weeks after your 3-week intensive are not your failure — they are an integration problem the entire pediatric disability care system has tolerated for decades. WithU absorbs the family labor that currently holds those 49 weeks together. Your LTV grows because the worldview of "lifelong relationship" is honored — not because we optimized your funnel.

We do not see you as customers to be sold SaaS to (you have enough SaaS). We see you as infrastructure partners whose participation in WithU is the data ecosystem's foundation. Your families bring WithU to you — not the other way around.

✦ OUR WORLDVIEW PROMISE TO FAMILIES
We see you as the world's foremost expert on your own child — not a patient, not a data entry worker, not a customer. The 15–25 hours per week you currently spend coordinating, translating, advocating, documenting — that labor is real, valuable, and unsustainable. WithU is built to absorb that labor, not to add to it. You curate; WithU integrates. You decide what to share; WithU never decides for you. You own this record; one day your child will own it from you.

We will never make you feel that your child is behind. We will never compare your child to typical norms in a way that judges. The reference frames are DS-normed. The framing is "different timeline, not delayed." Outcome data exists in the background; what reaches you at acute moments is dignity, deliverable, decision support.

✦ OUR WORLDVIEW PROMISE TO RESEARCHERS
We see research as a collaboration between scientists and the disability community — not as data extraction from passive subjects. Family voice is research-grade evidence, not survey noise. The three structurally invisible data dimensions (daily functioning, longitudinal QoL, caregiver experience) are invisible not because they don't exist, but because the research worldview hasn't valued them. WithU asks researchers to value what families know.

We do not see researchers as RWD customers to be sold cohort access (though pricing exists). We see researchers as mission partners whose work, properly grounded in family-reported real-world experience, will reshape what evidence-based pediatric disability care means. Co-authored publications. Foundation alignment. Family consent embedded from Day 1. This is research that respects everyone in the loop.

🧭 The single insight underlying all three canvases:

Families are the only entity that physically touches every provider, school, and home moment in a child's life. WithU is anchored to that fact. Provider VP relies on this (= we extend their reach beyond their walls). Family VP relies on this (= family is the curator, not the data source). Researcher VP relies on this (= we supply data they can't get elsewhere). If this single fact is not internalized, none of the three VPs hold.

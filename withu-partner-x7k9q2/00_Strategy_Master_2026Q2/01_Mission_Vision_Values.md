## The North Star — A Single Sentence

> **WithU exists to solve the structural integration deficit of pediatric disability care, by building the missing infrastructure that families have been forced to provide for free for decades.**

---

## Mission Statement

**WithU is the family-anchored, AI-native data infrastructure for the lifelong record of children with developmental disabilities.**

We exist because pediatric disability care is fragmented in a way no individual player can fix. Families touch 25–50 providers across 18 years. Each provider sees a slice. Outcome data doesn't exist. Equity gaps widen. Researchers struggle to recruit. Pharma can't develop pediatric drugs without RWD.

**We are the integration layer this industry has been waiting for.**

---

## Vision (5 Years)

By 2031, WithU is the de facto coordination infrastructure for pediatric disability care:

1. **100,000+ family records** active, owned by families
2. **Foundation partnerships** with NDSS, MDSC, and 5+ rare disease foundations
3. **Lighthouse Provider** alliances with NAPA, Sie Centers, and major academic medical centers
4. **Real-world data** powering 20+ academic publications and 1+ pharma orphan drug submission
5. **Industry standard** for cross-provider coordination, with API integrations across major EHR vendors
6. **Equity-driven access** through Foundation subsidies and employer benefits — disability families regardless of zip code or income have lifelong record access
7. **Cross-condition expansion** beyond DS to CP, autism, rare genetic syndromes — proven on the platform that started DS-anchored

## Values

The five values below are non-negotiable. They guide every strategic decision, every product trade-off, every hire.

### 1. Honest Realism

**We never claim what our MVP cannot back.**

- Mission statements wait until capability matches them
- Marketing is grounded in what families actually experience
- We tell investors and partners the truth, including risks
- "Aspirational" framings are clearly labeled as such
- We cite competitors by name and acknowledge their strengths

**Why:** The disability community has been over-promised by tech for decades. Families' trust is precious and easily lost.

### 2. Structural Defensibility

**We never enter direct competition with commodity SaaS.**

- We sit on top of existing tools (TheraNest, Cliniko, Epic) — never replace
- We integrate with patient portals — never compete with them
- We complement DSC2U and Foundation registries — never replicate
- We build moats through structural advantages (network effects, data accumulation, AI auto-import quality, Foundation partnerships)

**Why:** Commodity competition is a race to the bottom. Structural position creates lasting value.

### 3. User-First Absolute

**Family experience is non-negotiable.**

- Zero double-work is a hard requirement, enforced through AI auto-import architecture
- Family controls every share, every consent, every export
- Output-first design (acute moment deliverables) over feature-first design
- "Don't open every day" promise — the record grows passively while families live their lives
- Child's eventual ownership of their record is built into the architecture

**Why:** If WithU adds friction to a family's life, we have failed our mission, regardless of how much B2B revenue we generate.

### 4. Validation Discipline

**We never proceed on hypothesis alone.**

- Major strategic decisions require evidence (validation, pivot triggers)
- 30-day sprint cadence keeps assumptions tied to reality
- We talk to families, providers, researchers — not just each other
- We document our hypotheses explicitly, with pass/fail criteria
- We pivot when evidence demands, not when comfortable

**Why:** Pre-product founders fall in love with their assumptions. Discipline keeps us learning.

### 5. Strategic Patience

**We sequence to readiness, not to external calendars.**

- Foundation partnerships when we have product proof
- Senior intros when we have customer evidence
- Fundraising when capital deployment is clear
- Provider expansion when network density justifies
- Mission statements when capability matches them

**Why:** Khan Academy archetype. Credibility compounds over years. Rushing creates fragility.

## What WithU Is Not (Boundaries)

These boundaries are explicit founder commitments, not future considerations:

- **WithU is not a clinic EHR.** We do not compete with TheraNest, Fusion, Cliniko, Epic.
- **WithU is not a patient portal replacement.** We complement MyChart, Ocean Friends, Patient Gateway.
- **WithU is not a single-disease registry.** We span 30+ conditions, with DS as our anchor.
- **WithU is not a sales / marketing tool for providers.** We provide infrastructure for outcome documentation, not patient acquisition.
- **WithU is not a clinical decision-making engine.** We surface evidence and family voice; clinicians decide.
- **WithU is not an AI agent that acts autonomously on healthcare decisions.** Every consequential action is family-confirmed.
- **WithU is not US-only.** Founder is Tokyo-based; expansion to international markets is in our DNA from Day 1.

## Founder Statement

I am Ting Wen, the founder of WithU. I am the mother of a daughter with Down syndrome.

I started WithU not because I saw a market opportunity but because I lived inside the problem.

I have spent hundreds of hours coordinating between her pediatrician, her speech therapist, her physical therapist, her early intervention coordinator, her preschool, her cardiologist, and her ophthalmologist. None of them talked to each other. The integration was my job — unpaid, unrecognized, and occasionally, in moments of exhaustion, almost untenable.

When I realized this is not unique to Tokyo, not unique to my family, but is structural across pediatric disability care globally, I understood that this is not a problem any single player will solve. The structural integration deficit must be fixed by infrastructure that exists outside any single silo.

WithU is that infrastructure.

The discipline I bring is the discipline of a parent who knows that families do not have time for over-promises. We do not have time for tools that don't actually save us time. We do not have time for vendors that disappear in a year. We do not have time for products that fragment our attention.

WithU exists to honor that time.

We will build slowly when slowly is needed. We will build fast when families need speed. We will not over-promise. We will not pretend. We will sit on top of existing tools because that is where structural value lives. We will be honest with our community about what we cannot do.

And we will keep the integration deficit of pediatric disability care from being any family's job — by making it ours.

## Mission Lock — How We Stay True

The five values above can erode under pressure: investor demands, talent shortcuts, growth temptations, competitor moves. To prevent this:

1. **Founder governance:** I, as founder, hold a special class of share that protects core mission decisions for 7 years minimum
2. **Board structure:** 1+ board seat reserved for a disability community advocate (not investor)
3. **Annual community review:** Each year, an independent group of 5 disability families reviews WithU's strategic decisions for mission alignment
4. **Public commitments:** Core values published on website; we are accountable to families and partners
5. **Pivot transparency:** If we ever consider a pivot away from these values, it is publicly disclosed before the change

The five values exist to be enforced, not just stated.

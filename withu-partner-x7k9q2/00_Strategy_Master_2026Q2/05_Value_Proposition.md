## Core Value Proposition

**WithU is the family-anchored, AI-native data infrastructure for the lifelong record of children with developmental disabilities — solving the pediatric disability industry's structural integration deficit through three stakeholder-specific value propositions.**

## Profile 1: For Providers (NAPA, Hospital DSP, Specialty Clinics)

### Goal
**LTV (Lifetime Value) maximization** — measured by Revenue per patient × Visits per year × Years retained × Margin + Referrals.

### One-Sentence VP
> "WithU extends the relationship that begins in your clinic — turning each intensive into the start of a lifelong customer arc."

### The Problem (their reality)

Families come to NAPA-type intensive providers with **15 minutes of context** for a 3-week intensive. Cost: clinician time + bad-fit risk.

Between sessions, providers have **zero visibility into home life**. Are parents implementing what was taught? Or is the child regressing?

Families fly home, **run out of ideas by week 2**, and either don't return or fly elsewhere next year. **Each lost relationship is $5–15K of LTV.**

And outcome evidence stays anecdotal — hard to defend pricing, hard to win research, hard to convince payers.

### The Insight

Families are the only entity that physically touches every provider their child sees. **WithU is built around that fact.**

### The Promise

When they come to you: **real context**, not a 15-minute talk.

When they go home: **continuity** — your methodology travels with them.

Across the years: **lifelong customer arcs** — families return because the relationship continues.

### Pain → Pain Reliever

| Provider Pain | WithU Pain Reliever |
|---|---|
| 15-min intake / minimal context | Pre-arrival baseline + lifelong record summary delivered before family arrives |
| Zero visibility between sessions | Family-side capture flowing back to your clinical team |
| Week-2 idea exhaustion | Continuity bridge with home practice prompts |
| Families never return | Lifecycle architecture — preschool to adult, multiple touchpoints |
| Outcomes stay anecdotal | Cohort outcome data infrastructure with validated instruments |

### Gain → Gain Creator

| Provider Gain | WithU Gain Creator |
|---|---|
| Higher LTV | Lifelong customer arc + churn detection + return-visit re-engagement |
| Defensible pricing | Outcome evidence + cohort comparisons + research-grade data |
| "We know your child" differentiation | Real context at intake + cross-stage continuity |
| Research / academic positioning | IRB-ready exports + Foundation partnership infrastructure |
| Industry leadership | First Domino positioning across 400+ clinic ecosystem |

### What You Get (concrete)

1. **Higher patient LTV** — better pre-screening, retention, return visits across stages
2. **Better continuity** — therapists see home context, can adjust mid-course and post-discharge
3. **Differentiation** — you're the clinic that knows the child, not just the chart
4. **Evidence base** — for pricing, payer conversations, research, marketing
5. **Beyond your clinic** — positioning to lead the broader intensive-therapy ecosystem in evidence-driven care

### The Ask

- **Pilot:** 10–15 families × 3–6 months, free during pilot
- **Post-pilot pricing:** $20–30K/year per location, scaling with usage and outcomes demonstrated
- **Quarterly clinical review** with WithU team
- **Permission** to mention as a design partner (mutual visibility)
- **Optional:** warm intro to clinical lead for collaborative protocol design

### Why This Will Work for NAPA Specifically

- Bryan LaScala (CEO, marketing background) → "lifelong customer arc" framing speaks directly to LTV
- Lynette LaScala (CVO, founder, Cody's mother) → mission-aligned with family-first design
- 30+ disease intake categories → broad applicability of family-side capture
- Travel-based intensive model → continuity gap is structural and acute
- Self-pay pricing under pressure → outcome evidence directly supports defense

## Profile 2: For Families (Parents of Children with Developmental Disabilities)

### Goal
**Continuous growth support (Lifelong Growth Partnership)** — measured by right intervention at right moment × growth trajectory × parent confidence × child's eventual ownership.

### One-Sentence VP
> "WithU walks beside your child's growth — quietly capturing the journey so you have the right answer at every turning point."

### The Vivid Pitch

**You don't need to open WithU every day.**

When you meet a new doctor.
When an IEP is coming.
When life turns a corner.
When you have to decide.

That's when WithU shows up.

Three minutes. One page. Your child's full story.

**The rest of the time — please be with your child.**

We'll quietly grow the record in the background.

---

**Capture happens passively** — from photos you already take, voice notes you'd already make, emails already in your inbox.

**You curate; WithU integrates.**

You don't enter data. You receive deliverables.

---

**One day, your child will own this record.**

Until then, you're its keeper.

### Pain → Pain Reliever

| Family Pain | WithU Pain Reliever |
|---|---|
| **Repeating the same story to every provider (二度手間)** | **One-page summary in 3 minutes**, generated on demand |
| IEP comes and I have no evidence | **Advocacy packet** auto-built from 12 months of accumulated record |
| Stage transitions overwhelm me | **Transition portfolios** at age-appropriate moments |
| "Is this normal for DS?" judgment vacuum | **Cohort comparison + DS-specific reference frame** |
| My child's data stays in 5 different providers' systems | **Family-controlled, granular, revocable sharing** |
| Insurance denied my OT request | **Evidence portfolio** for appeals |
| I'm exhausted from being the integration point | **Capture-passive design** — no new entry moments |
| I want to share with grandma but it's too much | **Caregiver brief generator** — 1-pager for any audience |

### Gain → Gain Creator

| Family Gain | WithU Gain Creator |
|---|---|
| **Right answer at every turning point** | Episodic deliverables anchored to acute moments |
| **Cognitive load reduction** | Capture-passive design, no daily opening required |
| **"I'm the expert on my child"** | Curator role + advocate-grade evidence portfolio |
| **Lifelong continuity** | Lifecycle architecture (preschool → school → teen → adult) |
| **Child eventually owns their story** | Identity-aware UI evolving with the child as they mature |
| **Connection to similar families** | Cohort comparison creates implicit community |
| **Pride in my child's progress** | Auto-generated growth narratives for milestones |

### The 4 Acute Moments + 1 Backbone (framework core)

```
Backbone (always running quietly):
  └ Lifelong infrastructure: longitudinal record + family-controlled consent

Acute Moment 1: Provider Handoff (most frequent, monthly)
  ├ New provider visits
  └ Deliverable: 3-minute one-pager / caregiver brief

Acute Moment 2: Stage Transitions (1–3× per year)
  ├ EI → school, IEP start, adolescence, adult transition
  └ Deliverable: Transition portfolio / next-stage prep packet

Acute Moment 3: Decision Support (monthly+)
  ├ Intervention selection, frequency choices, second opinions
  └ Deliverable: Cohort comparison + evidence digest

Acute Moment 4: Advocacy (1–3× per year + ad hoc)
  ├ IEP preparation, insurance appeals, school negotiations
  └ Deliverable: Advocacy packet (evidence portfolio)
```

### What You Get (concrete)

1. **A 3-minute one-pager for every new provider** — never tell your child's story from scratch again
2. **An IEP-ready evidence packet** — built from 12 months of accumulated observations
3. **A growing record of your child's life** — owned by you, transferable to your child later
4. **Quiet support during transitions** — preschool → school → teen → adult, the system follows
5. **Cohort comparisons** — see how similar children's trajectories evolved, without the anxiety of "averages"

### The Promise (pricing model)

- **Free for families** through provider partnerships (Wave 1 model)
- **Direct family subscription** as backup for families whose providers don't partner (Wave 2 future)
- **Always:** family owns the data, family controls sharing, family can export and leave at any time

### The Implicit Bargain

> "We know you don't have time. We know you don't want another app. So we promise: your effort will be near-zero, but the value will arrive at the moments that matter most. Test us once at an IEP, once at a new doctor — and judge us by whether the deliverable actually saved you."

This is the **anti-app pitch**: we ask for less than any other app. We deliver more than any other app at the moments where you're most stretched.

## Profile 3: For Researchers (Academic, Foundation, Pharma RWD)

### Goal
**Real-World Data (RWD) supply** — measured by data depth × breadth × longitudinality × uniqueness × regulatory alignment.

### One-Sentence VP
> "WithU supplies the data category researchers can't get from clinics, claims, or surveys — what families capture in daily life."

### The Vivid Pitch

**You already have:**
- Clinical data (from your clinic visits)
- Claims data (from insurance)
- Your existing registry (snapshot enrollment data)

**What you don't have:**

What's happening when the child is at home.

---

WithU supplies three categories of data that are **structurally invisible** to traditional research:

#### 1. Real-World Daily Functioning
Video, voice, observations from actual life — not 60-minute clinic visits.
- Standardized capture prompts that match GMFM-88, PEDI, PODCI items
- Performance in the kitchen, the playground, the dinner table
- Context (people, environment, time of day) attached to every observation

#### 2. Longitudinal Quality of Life
Family-reported, captured continuously — not snapshot surveys.
- PROMs administered with low burden (1–2 minutes monthly, not 45 minutes annually)
- Parent QoL, child QoL, sibling QoL when applicable
- Trajectories, not single scores

#### 3. Caregiver Experience
Parental stress, decision-making confidence, advocacy outcomes — a domain academic research has historically ignored.
- Time burden quantification
- IEP outcomes (won / lost / negotiated)
- Decision regret / confidence
- Mental health trajectory

### Why This Is Structurally Different

Because **families are the data source**, not clinics, you get:
- **Phenotypically diverse multi-site data** — without site-by-site recruitment
- **Low family burden** — capture-passive design, not survey blasts
- **High follow-up retention** — families stay because they get value too
- **Family-controlled granular consent** — IRB-friendly from day one

### Pain → Pain Reliever

| Researcher Pain | WithU Pain Reliever |
|---|---|
| Recruitment slow & expensive ($200–2,000/family) | **Family-as-integration-node** = no clinic-by-clinic recruitment |
| Longitudinal attrition 30–50% at 6 months | **Capture-passive design** = retention >80% |
| Phenotype data fragmented across clinics | **Family-curated** integrates across sites |
| Foundation registries: low engagement, one-time enrollment | **Engaging registry infrastructure** white-label |
| HIPAA / IRB friction in data sharing | **Family-controlled granular consent** |
| Survey burden burns out research participants | **Passive capture** + low-friction PROMs |

### Gain → Gain Creator

| Researcher Gain | WithU Gain Creator |
|---|---|
| **FDA-aligned RWD** | Family-side capture of daily functioning |
| **Multi-site federated analyses** | Standardized data structure across sites |
| **Longitudinal QoL trajectory** | Continuous family reporting, not snapshots |
| **Cross-condition comparative research** | Common infrastructure for DS, CP, autism, rare genetic |
| **Foundation collaboration hub** | White-label registry SaaS |
| **Pharma RWD partnership pathway** | Regulatory-ready data structure |
| **AI / ML training datasets** | Consented, structured, longitudinal pediatric data |

### What You Get (concrete)

1. **Three data categories no one else has** — daily functioning, longitudinal QoL, caregiver experience
2. **Multi-site data without multi-site recruitment cost** — family-as-node = phenotypic diversity for free
3. **High retention** — capture-passive design keeps families engaged for years
4. **IRB-ready exports** — family-controlled consent simplifies governance
5. **Foundation registry white-label option** — for Foundations that lack technical infrastructure

### The Ask

- **Pilot research IRB protocol** — co-developed with WithU; we run the family-side
- **50–100 families enrolled** in initial cohort
- **Co-author publications** — aligned to Foundation / academic mission
- **Federated analysis tooling co-development** — for multi-site future
- **Pricing varies by use case:**
  - Grant-funded research: cost-recovery
  - Foundation registry: revenue share or annual fee
  - Pharma RWD: market-rate licensing
  - Academic publications: free (mission-aligned)

### The Implicit Bargain

> "Your existing data sources serve you well for what they were built for. We're not replacing them. We're filling the gap they were never built for: real-world life, family voice, caregiver experience. Three structurally invisible dimensions. Captured at scale, with family consent, IRB-friendly from day one."

This is the **gap-filler pitch**: we don't compete with your existing infrastructure, we add the layer that's missing.

## Cross-Cutting: The Single Insight

Underlying all three Value Propositions is one structural truth:

> **Families are the only entity that physically touches every provider, school, and home moment in a child's life. WithU is anchored to that fact.**

This sentence is the **structural key** that:
- Provider VP relies on (= why we can extend their reach beyond their walls)
- Parent VP relies on (= why family is the curator, not the data source)
- Researcher VP relies on (= why we can supply data they can't get elsewhere)

If this sentence is not internalized, none of the three VPs hold.

---

## Cross-Cutting: The Three Mechanisms

```
Mechanism 1: Family's continuous existence is captured
  → Provider: relationship continuity beyond intensive
  → Parent: child's life continuously held
  → Researcher: longitudinal trajectory, low attrition

Mechanism 2: Acute moments trigger deliverables
  → Provider: pre/post intensive bridge artifacts
  → Parent: 3-minute summaries, IEP packets
  → Researcher: study-specific exports on demand

Mechanism 3: Family-side capture is reused across stakeholders
  → Same data, three audiences, three forms
  → Structural efficiency that creates the moat
```

## What WithU Explicitly Does NOT Do

To be sharp on positioning, here's what WithU does NOT promise — even though adjacent products do:

| WithU does NOT | Why |
|---|---|
| Replace clinic EHRs (Cliniko, WebPT, NextGen) | We sit on top, don't replace |
| Replace patient portals (Ocean Friends, MyChart) | We add the lifelong / cross-provider layer |
| Generate clinical recommendations / prescriptions | Liability + scope; we surface evidence, providers decide |
| Solve insurance reimbursement directly | We supply documentation; payers decide |
| Replace research registries entirely | We complement; we add real-world data |
| Compete with single-disease apps (DSC2U, etc.) | We span 30+ conditions, family-anchored |
| Solve school IEP politics | We supply evidence; parents advocate |

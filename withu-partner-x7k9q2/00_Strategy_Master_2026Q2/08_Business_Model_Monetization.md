## Business Model Architecture

WithU follows a **B2C-led with B2B network effects** structure:

- **Primary user:** Family (free at Day 1, freemium long-term)
- **Revenue sources:** Multiple B2B paths (Foundation, Employer, Pharma, Academic, State)
- **Data ecosystem:** Family + Providers (voluntary lighthouse + involuntary via family rights) + Schools / EI

This mirrors Plaid (consumers don't pay; fintechs do), Picnic Health (patients access data; pharma pays), and Folia Health (caregivers track; foundations + research pay).

**Key insight:** Data ecosystem participants are NOT the same as revenue payers. This separation is structurally important and validates our defensibility model.

## Six B2B Revenue Paths

WithU has 6 distinct B2B revenue paths, each with different decision makers, ROI logic, and timelines.

### Phase × Revenue Mix

## Revenue Path 1: Foundation Registry Infrastructure

### Decision Maker
- **Executive Director / President** of Foundation
- **Director of Programs / Member Services**
- **Board of Directors** (mission alignment)

### What They Care About
1. Member retention / engagement
2. Mission impact (family support quality)
3. Research data quality (for grants)
4. Operating efficiency
5. Donor visibility / fundraising stories

### Why They Pay

> "Your members get a powerful, free tool that organizes their child's health journey. You get the structured data that turns member touchpoints into research-grade evidence — without your team having to build or maintain anything."

Specifically:
- Member value-add → retention boost
- Research-grade data → grant approval rate up
- Family engagement → community strengthening
- Donor-compelling stories (with data)
- Operational: replace ad-hoc registry with professional system

### Current Alternative Cost

| Alternative | Cost | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| **DIY registry build** | $50-200K initial + $50K/year maintenance | Tech capacity gaps, low engagement |
| **REDCap / vendor registry** | $20-50K/year + internal staff | One-time enrollment only, low engagement |
| **No registry** | $0 cost | Lost research, lost members, lost donors |

### Dollar-Backed ROI

For a mid-size Foundation (5,000 members):

```
WithU cost:
  - Initial pilot: free (6 months)
  - Year 1: $30-50K subsidy + per-member fee
  - Year 2+: $100-200K (5,000 members × $30-50/family/year)

Foundation benefit:
  Member retention + 1.6x adherence (DSC2U validated)
   → visible member value
  Research data → grant approval rate +15-30% (estimated)
   → year 1 additional grant value $300-500K
  Donor visibility → annual giving +5-10%
   → $50-200K additional donations
  Operating efficiency → manual processing reduction
   → $20-50K savings

Net: $100-200K investment for $370-750K returns (Year 2-3)
```

### Common Objections + Handling

| Objection | Handling |
|---|---|
| "We lack technical evaluation capacity" | Pilot demonstrates, technical details abstracted |
| "Privacy / HIPAA concerns" | Family-controlled consent + BAA + 3rd-party audit |
| "Our members are diverse, will it serve all?" | Multi-condition, stage-adaptive design covers diversity |
| "What if you go out of business?" | Family-controlled data + open export functionality |
| "Foundation budget is limited" | Pilot is free, paid only after ROI confirmed |

### Sales Conversation

**Opener (30 sec):**
> "Your Foundation is competing for member benefits, you need a registry, but build/maintain is heavy. We provide the infrastructure — families use it free, your Foundation gains member engagement and research data, your team does nothing."

**Value Reveal (90 sec):**
- Family experience demo
- Foundation dashboard preview (member metrics)
- Research export sample
- Total cost vs alternative comparison

**Ask (30 sec):**
> "Pilot 100 families, 6 months, free. Together we look at engagement metrics. Six months later, evaluate paid model. We provide the materials to propose to your board."

### Deal Size & Timing

| Stage | ARR | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Pilot (free)** | $0 | 6 months |
| **Year 1** | $30-100K | small Foundation |
| **Year 2-3** | $100-500K | scale to all members |
| **Sales cycle** | 6-12 months | Pilot → board approval → contract |

### Phase Fit
- **Phase 1**: Pilot 1-2 Foundations
- **Phase 2**: Scale to 3-5 Foundations
- **Phase 3**: Portfolio of 5-15 Foundations

## Revenue Path 2: Employer / Insurance Benefit

### Decision Maker
- **CHRO / VP Total Rewards / Benefits Director**
- **DEI Officer** (diversity benefits context)
- **CFO** (cost / retention evaluation)
- **Benefits broker** (Mercer, Aon, WTW — major channel partners)

### What They Care About
1. **Employee retention** (especially senior talent)
2. **DEI / inclusion metrics**
3. **Healthcare cost containment**
4. **Benefit differentiation** in talent market
5. **Productivity** (distraction reduction, PTO reduction)

### Why They Pay

> "Many of your senior parent employees have special needs children, and they leave for inadequate support. WithU is an employer-paid benefit that lets these parents focus at work. DEI signal, retention boost, and productivity gain — all in one."

### Current Alternative Cost

| Alternative | Cost / family / month |
|---|---|
| **Cleo Health** (pediatric / parenting) | $50-150 |
| **Maven Clinic** (women / family) | $20-100 |
| **Care.com Benefits** | $20-100 |
| **No special needs-specific benefit** | $0 cost, but huge turnover risk |

→ Cleo / Maven lack special needs specialty — unmet market.

### Dollar-Backed ROI

For 500-employee company (estimated 5-10 special needs families):

```
WithU cost: $30-50/family/month × 10 families × 12 months = $3.6-6K/year

Cost savings per retained senior employee:
  $50-200K (recruitment cost) + $30-100K (onboarding cost)
  
Productivity gain per supported family:
  $10-30K/year (PTO + distraction reduction)
  × 10 families = $100-300K/year

Net ROI:
  Investment: $5K
  Return (conservative): $200-500K/year
  ROI: 40-100x
```

### Common Objections + Handling

| Objection | Handling |
|---|---|
| "We already have Cleo / Maven" | "Those are general; WithU is specialty depth. Complementary." |
| "Our population doesn't have many" | "Actually 5-10% of employees are disability families, just hidden." |
| "Privacy concerns" | "Employer sees only usage stats; family data is family-controlled." |
| "Hard to measure ROI" | "Cleo published case studies + WithU pilot data." |
| "Budget already committed" | "Next benefits cycle proposal, ROI calculation prepared." |

### Sales Path

```
Channel 1: Benefits Broker (Mercer, Aon, WTW)
  → Single broker accesses 100+ companies

Channel 2: Direct DEI conversation
  → Approach DEI lead directly

Channel 3: Benefits marketplace (Bswift, Workday Benefits)
  → Marketplace listing for relevant companies

Recommended: Channel 1 + 2 in parallel
```

### Deal Size & Timing

| Stage | ARR | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Pilot** | $5-10K (1 quarter) | 3 months |
| **Year 1 small employer** (500-1000 emp) | $10-30K | 12 months |
| **Year 2 mid employer** (5K-50K emp) | $50-200K | scale |
| **Enterprise large** (50K+) | $200K-1M | annual |
| **Sales cycle** | 9-18 months | Benefits annual cycle |

### Phase Fit
- **Phase 2 start**: 1 pilot
- **Phase 2 end**: 3-5 employers
- **Phase 3**: Marketplace listing, 10+ employers

## Revenue Path 3: Pharma RWD Licensing

### Decision Maker
- **Head of Real-World Evidence / RWD**
- **Medical Affairs Director**
- **Clinical Development VP**
- **HEOR / Market Access**

### What They Care About
1. **Data quality** for FDA / regulatory submission
2. **Patient access** for trials and post-market
3. **Clinical insights** for drug development
4. **Post-market surveillance** efficiency
5. **HEOR** (Health Economics & Outcomes Research)

### Why They Pay

> "DS / rare disease RWD sources are limited. Pediatric specifically is scarce. Family-anchored data captures real-world functioning. Caregiver experience domain is academically ignored. Your drug development, regulatory submission, and post-market surveillance need this unique source."

### Current Alternative Cost

| Alternative | Cost |
|---|---|
| **Custom prospective study** | $1-5M for cohort of 200-500 |
| **Aetion / IQVIA RWD** (claims-based) | $500K-2M for limited datasets |
| **Patient registry data** | $100K-500K |
| **Disease-specific data brokers** | $200K-1M |
| **Picnic Health rare disease data** | $500K-2M |

### Dollar-Backed ROI

For DS treatment development pharma:

```
WithU dataset cost: $1-3M for cohort N=2,000 longitudinal
  vs.
Custom prospective study: $5-15M for similar N
  vs.
Existing Aetion: not pediatric-deep enough

Pharma value:
  - Phase 3 trial accelerated by 6-12 months → $millions in revenue
  - Stronger regulatory submission → higher approval probability
  - Post-marketing surveillance → market access negotiation
  - HEOR studies → reimbursement justification

Net ROI: $1-3M investment for $10M-100M acceleration value
```

### Deal Size & Timing

| Deal Type | ARR / Total | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Custom dataset license** | $500K-2M one-time | 6-9 months |
| **Annual subscription** | $1-5M/year | Multi-year contract |
| **Per-study collaboration** | $200K-1M | per study |
| **Sales cycle** | 12-24 months | Pharma internal procurement |

### Phase Fit
- **Phase 1-2**: Foundation base building (cohort N expansion)
- **Phase 3**: First Pharma deal (Year 3 target)
- **Phase 4**: Pharma portfolio (5+ pharma deals)

## Revenue Path 4: Academic Research Collaboration

### Decision Maker
- **Principal Investigator (PI)**
- **Research Director / Center Director**
- **Department Head** (institutional sign-off)

### What They Care About
1. **Grant funding** (NIH, foundation, industry)
2. **Publication impact**
3. **Patient cohort access**
4. **Methodology rigor**
5. **Career advancement**

### Why They Pay (or Partner)

> "Recruitment cost is your largest grant expense, and 50% drop at 6 months kills retention. We provide engaged longitudinal cohort. Co-authored publications accelerate your publication rate, increase grant success."

### Dollar-Backed ROI

For NIH R01 study (5 year, $2.5M):

```
WithU cost: $50-200K for cohort access
  vs.
Without WithU:
  - Recruitment: $200K-1M
  - Setup: $100K
  - Lost retention → underpowered → potential failure

Win:
  Grant approval probability up
  Faster execution → Year 4 publication (vs Year 6)
  Multi-site federated → larger N → higher impact
  Career impact for PI

ROI: $50-200K investment for $300K-1M grant savings
```

### Deal Size & Timing

| Stage | Total | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| **Single grant collaboration** | $50-200K | 12-18 months |
| **Multi-year partnership** | $100-300K/year | 3-5 years |
| **Institution-wide subscription** | $200K-1M/year | annual renewal |
| **Sales cycle** | 6-18 months | grant timing |

## Strategic Implications

### For Investors
Multi-stakeholder revenue creates **resilience**: failure of one path doesn't kill the company. Phased revenue plan creates **predictability**: each phase has explicit ARR targets.

### For Operators
Each path has different sales cycles, decision makers, and persuasion materials. We need **specialized BD per channel** by Phase 3+.

### For Mentors
We are not building "another B2C app." We are building infrastructure with **6 distinct B2B revenue paths**, each justified by structural value, not feature competition.

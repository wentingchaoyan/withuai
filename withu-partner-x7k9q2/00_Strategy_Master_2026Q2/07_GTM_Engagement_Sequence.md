## GTM Philosophy

WithU enters each player segment through their most acute Job-to-Be-Done (the wedge), then expands. The order is determined by **dependency chains** and **network effects**.

### Sequence Decision Principles

1. **Upstream-first dependency** — If Player A's adoption is a prerequisite for Player B, A goes first
2. **JTBD urgency order** — Players with most acute pain are most likely to adopt
3. **Network effect catalyst order** — Players who pull other players in go first
4. **Risk-reward optimization** — Lower-risk validation before higher-risk commitments

```
Wave 0  (Months 0–6)   : Family Direct (B2C foundation)
Wave 1  (Months 6–12)  : Foundation Anchor (advocacy partner)

Wave 7  (Months 36–48) : State Agency / Financial (platform expansion)
```

## Wave 0: Family Direct (Months 0–6) ★ Foundation of Everything

### Why First?
- Families are the **integration node** of WithU's entire ecosystem
- No network effects can activate without family adoption
- B2C product viability is independently testable

### Family JTBD (Strategyn Format)

**Functional Jobs:**
1. F1: Prepare for new provider visits (monthly)
2. F2: Prepare for IEP / IFSP meetings (1–3× per year)
3. F3: Retrieve information needed for medical decisions (multiple times monthly)
4. F4: Share status with grandparents / caregivers (multiple times monthly)
5. F5: Collect documents for insurance / benefits applications (5–10× per year)

**Emotional Jobs:**
- E1: Reduce decision fatigue
- E2: Maintain "I am the expert on my child" self-efficacy
- E3: Reduce isolation as a disability parent

**Social Jobs:**
- S1: Be recognized as professional advocate in formal settings
- S2: Be valued in DS family community

### WithU's Wedge — Entering Through the Most Acute JTBD

```
ENTRY: F1 (New provider preparation)
  → "3-minute one-pager" output-first deliverable
  → Monthly usage, acute moment resolution
  → AI auto-import building record in background

EXPANSION: F2 → F5, E1-E3
  → IEP packets, medical decision briefs, family sharing, advocacy portfolios
  → New deliverables for each acute moment
  → Backend record deepens
```

### Outcome Statements (Family Wants)

```
Minimize:
  - Times re-explaining the same story to new providers
  - Time spent on IEP preparation
  - "I don't remember what was said" moments
  - Time spent searching for documents

Maximize:
  - Quality time with child
  - "I am the expert on my child" feeling
  - Service hours secured at IEPs
  - Inter-family sync
```

## Wedge JTBD Selection Logic

### Why Enter Through Most Acute Pain Per Player?

```
When players consider switching to a new tool:
  Switching cost > Value gap → They don't adopt

WithU is NOT "exhaustively comprehensive" — it overwhelms
1 specific JTBD as the wedge:

Example: Family F1 (new provider preparation)
  - Occurs monthly (high frequency)
  - But acute (resolution required immediately)
  - No existing solution or insufficient
  - WithU's output (1-page summary) is immediate value
  
→ Other JTBDs (F2-F5) are expansion via same infrastructure
→ Family trusts WithU through the wedge
```

### Expansion Order Logic

```
1. Solve wedge JTBD overwhelmingly (build trust)
2. Backend record naturally grows (infrastructure complete)
3. Same data enables other JTBD deliverables
4. Family discovers "this is also useful," deeper engagement
5. Network effects activate (Foundation, Provider, Researcher)
```

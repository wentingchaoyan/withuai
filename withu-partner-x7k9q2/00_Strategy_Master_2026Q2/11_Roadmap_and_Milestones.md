**Version:** 1.0 · 2026-05-08

## Roadmap Philosophy

WithU sequences engagement to **WithU's readiness, not external calendars**. Each phase has defined entry criteria (validation), exit criteria (deliverables ready), and capital + team milestones.

We use the **8-Wave engagement sequence** as the primary structure. Each wave maps to a phase of company maturation.

---

| Phase | Period | Wave Focus | ARR Target | Team Size | Capital |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Critical Path Dependencies

The 8-Wave engagement sequence has critical path dependencies:

```
Family Adoption (Wave 0) ──┐
   │                       │
   ↓                       │
Foundation Pilot (Wave 1)  │
   │                       │
   ↓                       ↓
Researcher (Wave 2)    NAPA Lighthouse (Wave 1.5)
   │                       │
   ↓                       │
Local Therapy (Wave 2.5)   │
   │                       │
   ├────────────────────  Hospital DSP (Wave 3) ←
   │                       │                     │
   ↓                       ↓                     │
School/EI (Wave 4)    Employer Benefit (Wave 5)  │
                           │                     │
                           ↓                     ↓
                       Pharma RWD (Wave 6)  State (Wave 7)
```

**If Wave 0 fails** (family adoption), all subsequent waves fail.
**If Wave 1 fails** (Foundation), Wave 2/3 still possible but slower.
**If Wave 1.5 fails** (NAPA), brand alignment lost, but other waves continue.

## Risk-Adjusted Timeline

The dates above assume successful validation at each phase. Risk-adjusted scenarios:

### Optimistic (60% probability)
Phase 1 completes in 9 months instead of 12. Phase 2-5 accelerate accordingly. 60% probability assumes:
- AI auto-import works as expected
- MDSC partnership signs in May 2026
- NAPA Bryan responds positively
- Series A closes by Q4 2026 (before Phase 1 end)

### Expected (30% probability)
Phase 1 completes in 12-15 months. Phase 2-5 stretch by ~3 months. Standard execution risks materialize.

### Pessimistic (10% probability)
Phase 1 stretches to 18 months. Major pivot required mid-Phase 1. Phase 2-5 timing significantly impacted. This scenario requires:
- Loop problem invalidation
- Foundation pilots rejection or delay
- Folia DS specialty pivot
- Capital tightness

In pessimistic scenario: pivot to alternative approach (B2C-only, or specialty-only, or international-led).

## Roadmap as Mission

This roadmap is not just a financial or operational plan — it is a **mission timeline**. Each phase brings the disability community closer to a future where:

- No family is forced to be the unpaid integration coordinator
- Outcome data exists for evidence-based care
- Equity gaps narrow because access is not zip-code-dependent
- Research advances because data flows
- Pediatric drugs reach approval because RWD exists
- Policy reflects reality because evidence is structural

Every milestone above is in service of this. Every dollar of revenue is in service of this. Every team member's work is in service of this.

The roadmap is the path. The destination is a structurally fixed industry.

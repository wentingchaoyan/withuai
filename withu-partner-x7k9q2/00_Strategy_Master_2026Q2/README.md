2. **Structural Defensibility** — never enter direct commodity competition
3. **User-First Absolute** — never compromise on family experience (zero double-work)

- Quarterly board review (next: Aug 2026)
- 30-day sprint validation cycles
- Major pivots trigger document refresh

## Quick Strategic Summary

> **WithU is the Pediatric Plaid — the missing infrastructure for the structurally fragmented pediatric disability care industry. We are family-anchored, AI-native, cross-provider, lifelong, and pediatric-specialty. We solve the integration deficit no individual player has incentive or capability to solve. Our Phase 1 wedge is family adoption + Foundation partnership; Phase 2 expands to Researcher and Local Therapy passive participation; Phase 3 unlocks Pharma RWD and Hospital DSP partnerships; Phase 4 scales to State Agency contracts. The 4-element combination — pediatric specialty depth × cross-provider integration × lifelong arc × AI auto-import — has no incumbent today.**

## The Big Idea in 30 Seconds

**Pediatric disability care is structurally fragmented.** A child with Down syndrome touches 25–50 providers across 18 years — clinics, schools, therapists, specialists, intensive programs — none of which talk to each other. Families are forced into the role of unpaid integration coordinators, while research data, outcome evidence, and equity all suffer.

**WithU is the missing infrastructure.** We are the family-anchored, AI-native data integration layer — the "Pediatric Plaid" — that makes the fragmented system functional. Families finally get a tool that captures their child's life across all providers, with zero double-work. Foundations, researchers, and pharma get access to the unified longitudinal data that has been impossible to aggregate.

**No incumbent has all four ingredients we have:** (1) Pediatric specialty depth, (2) Cross-provider integration, (3) Lifelong arc, (4) AI auto-import.

---

## The Industry Problem

Pediatric disability care has 10 structural problems that **no individual player can solve alone**:

1. **Continuity gap** — Intensive interventions (3-week bursts) lose value during the 49-week off-period
2. **Expertise concentration** — DS specialty exists at only 10–15 centers; 95% of families lack access
3. **Knowledge fragmentation** — Each provider sees their slice; no one has the full picture
4. **Outcome opacity** — No one can measure what actually works long-term
5. **Family burnout** — Parents spend 15–25 hours/week on uncompensated coordination
6. **Regulatory inefficiency** — Insurance, IEP, Medicaid all require scattered evidence
7. **Research data gap** — Despite millions of children, we have weak data on what interventions work
8. **Equity gap** — Engaged suburban parents access 10× more services than rural / lower-income peers
9. **Stage transitions crisis** — Every transition (EI→school, etc.) loses 50%+ of context
10. **Cost spiral** — Pediatric disability costs are rising faster than coverage; family bankruptcies common

This is a **tragedy of the commons**: integration is everyone's problem and no one's job.

**Why it persists:** Each player's business model is bounded by their silo. Crossing the silo creates costs without revenue. No one — not providers, not insurers, not foundations, not research labs — has the incentive *and* capability to solve this.

## Why Now (5 Enablers Aligned)

1. **AI Maturity (2023+)** — LLMs and multimodal AI now extract structure from unstructured family data (emails, photos, voice). Pre-AI, this required manual user input — too high friction for exhausted parents.

2. **Regulatory Tailwind (2016+)** — 21st Century Cures Act, HITECH Act, and Information Blocking Rules give families legal rights to all of their child's medical and educational data. WithU operates on user-authorized access (the Plaid model for healthcare).

3. **Foundation Maturity** — DSC2U (Skotko), INCLUDE Project (NIH), and major DS Foundations are now actively investing in data infrastructure. The cultural readiness for tech partnership exists.

4. **Pharma RWD Demand** — FDA's RWD guidance (2018+) and orphan drug development for rare pediatric diseases create a high-margin market for WithU's data. Pediatric specialty data is currently scarce.

5. **Founder-Market Fit** — Tokyo-based founder Ting Wen brings a unique combination: parent of a child with Down syndrome, Foundation network access, and tech / startup background. Past founders had domain *or* tech, not both.

## What WithU Is (and Is Not)

### What WithU Is

- **A family-anchored lifelong record** for children with developmental disabilities (DS, CP, autism, rare syndromes)
- **An AI-native data integration layer** that auto-imports from emails, photos, voice memos, and patient portals
- **A cross-provider infrastructure** spanning intensive programs, hospitals, local therapy, schools, and EI agencies
- **A Foundation-aligned platform** for member benefits, registry infrastructure, and research partnerships
- **A research-grade RWD source** for academic and pharma RWD applications

### What WithU Is Not

- **Not a clinic EHR** (we don't compete with TheraNest, Cliniko, Epic)
- **Not a patient portal** (we don't compete with MyChart, Ocean Friends)
- **Not a single-disease app** (we span 30+ conditions)
- **Not a school IEP system** (we don't compete with EasyIEP, Frontline)
- **Not a sales tool for intensive providers** (we provide infrastructure, not marketing)

WithU enters each player segment through their most acute Job-to-Be-Done (the wedge), then expands. The order is determined by dependency chains and network effects.

## Business Model — Multi-Stakeholder Revenue

WithU's data ecosystem (where data flows) is **distinct** from our revenue sources (who pays). This mirrors Plaid (consumers don't pay; fintechs do) and Picnic Health (patients access data; pharma pays).

### Phase 1 (Months 0–12): Foundation Pilot
- Free for families
- 1–2 Foundation pilots → $50–200K ARR

### Phase 2 (Months 12–24): Foundation Scale + Employer + Academic
- Foundation per-family fees ($30–50/year)
- Employer benefit pilots ($30–50/family/month, employer pays)
- Academic co-grants ($50–200K each)
- Total: $300K–1M ARR

### Phase 3 (Months 24–36): Pharma + State
- Pharma RWD licensing ($500K–2M per dataset)
- State agency contracts ($500K–2M/year)
- Total: $1–3M ARR

### Phase 4 (Months 36–60): All Channels Mature
- Total: $5–15M ARR

**Capital required:**
- Seed (Months 0–12): $1–2M
- Series A (Months 12–24): $5–10M
- Series B (Months 24–36): $20–50M
- Series C (Months 36–60): $50–100M+

## Defensibility (Moat Strategy)

WithU's defensibility is built on **structural advantages, not features**:

1. **Network effects** — Each family connecting to N providers creates value for those providers; switching cost grows quadratically
2. **Data accumulation** — Longitudinal data over 5+ years cannot be replicated by new entrants
3. **Foundation partnerships** — Becoming the registry infrastructure for major foundations creates institutional lock-in
4. **AI auto-import IP** — Our extraction quality compounds with our data
5. **Trust** — Once a family entrusts their child's lifelong record, switching is unthinkable
